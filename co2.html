CO₂ FIRE FIGHTING SYSTEM

Professional CO₂ Fire Suppression System Service

Citra Marine Services International provides inspection, servicing and maintenance of fixed CO₂ fire extinguishing systems installed onboard vessels.

Our services cover CO₂ cylinders, release mechanisms, piping, discharge nozzles and related components to support system readiness and marine fire safety requirements.

WHAT WE PROVIDE

OUR CO₂ SYSTEM SERVICES

01

CO₂ Cylinder Inspection

Inspection of CO₂ cylinders, identification, physical condition and filling status according to applicable procedures.

02

Fixed CO₂ System Inspection

Inspection of fixed CO₂ fire extinguishing system installations, manifolds, connections and associated components.

03

Release & Control System

Inspection of release cabinets, activation mechanisms, control arrangements and related components according to system configuration.

04

Piping & Discharge Nozzles

Inspection of discharge piping, connections and nozzles to assess their condition and suitability for service.

05

Testing & Maintenance

System testing and maintenance in accordance with applicable requirements, manufacturer instructions and approved procedures.

06

Certificate & Service Report

Preparation of service reports and issuance of certificates based on completed work, inspection results and applicable authorization.
